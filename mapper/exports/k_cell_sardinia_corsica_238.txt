(-238) (-238 or -237) Rebel Punic mercenaries on Sardinia are driven out by the natives to Italy; they appeal to Rome for the second time (she had refused them earlier) and she reverses her previous decision and occupies Sardinia before Carthaginians can send a force to do so herself; Rome threatens war if she resists, so Carthage acquiesces to the loss, with bitterness, surrendering Sardinia and paying a further 1,200 talents. Rome also annexes Corsica (AI), though Corsica is not named in the treaty (NCAH VII_2.11 p 569) (CAH VII.25 pp 804-5) (Wiki "Mercenary War" 11/24/26: probably 237) (Wiki "Sardinia and Corsica" 11/24/26: 238).

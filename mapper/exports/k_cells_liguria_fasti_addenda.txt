(-236) The consul P. Cornelius Lentulus Caudinus campaigns in the Ligurian mountains, said to have stormed some forts (perhaps only a show of strength), and wins the first triumph over the Ligurians (CAH VII.25 p 806) (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26).
(-233) The consul Q. Fabius Maximus Verrucosus defeats the Ligurians and triumphs on 1 February, ending the wars (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26, citing Dyson 1985 pp 94-95) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26); NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3 has the campaigns lasting to 230.

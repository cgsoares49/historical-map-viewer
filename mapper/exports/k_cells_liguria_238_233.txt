(-238) (-238 to -233 or -230) Romans begin annual campaigns in Liguria against mountain tribes, lasting until -233 or -230; the consul Ti. Sempronius Gracchus fights them in 238 (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3: campaigns 238 to 230) (CAH VII.25 pp 805-6) (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26: wars end 233, citing Dyson 1985 pp 94-95).
(-237) The Roman campaign in Liguria is repeated but broken off early when the consular armies turn against the Boii after the Gallic raid on Ariminum (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3).
(-236) The consul P. Cornelius Lentulus campaigns in the Ligurian mountains, said to have stormed some forts (perhaps only a show of strength), and wins the first triumph over the Ligurians (CAH VII.25 p 806) (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26).
(-235) Roman consular campaign against the Ligurians continues; no commander or action is recorded (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3: campaigns continue intermittently) (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26).
(-234) Roman consular campaign against the Ligurians continues; no commander or action is recorded (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3) (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26).
(-233) The consul Q. Fabius Maximus (Verrucosus) defeats the Ligurians and celebrates a triumph, ending the wars (Wiki "Ligures" 11/24/26, citing Dyson 1985 pp 94-95); NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3 has the campaigns lasting to 230.

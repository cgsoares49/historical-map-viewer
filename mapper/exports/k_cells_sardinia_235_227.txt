(-235) Sardinians resist Roman rule and are put down by the consul T. Manlius Torquatus, who triumphs over them on 10 March (CAH VII.25 p 805) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26).
(-234) The consul Sp. Carvilius Maximus triumphs over the Sardinians on 1 April (CAH VII.25 p 805) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26).
(-233) The consul M'. Pomponius Matho triumphs over the Sardinians on 15 March (CAH VII.25 p 805) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26).
(-231) Two consular armies are sent against the islands, C. Papirius Maso against the Corsicans and M'. Pomponius Matho against the Sardinians; Papirius triumphs over the Corsicans on 5 March, the first triumph held on the Alban Mount, and none is recorded for Pomponius (CAH VII.25 p 805) (Wiki "Fasti Triumphales" 11/24/26).
(-227) Sardinia and Corsica are made a province like Sicily: the praetors are doubled to four and Marcus Valerius is assigned Sardinia and Corsica (C. Flaminius takes Sicily), and tithes are collected from all the tribes (NCAH VII_2.11 p 571) (NCAH VII_2.9 p 439) (CAH VII.25 p 805).

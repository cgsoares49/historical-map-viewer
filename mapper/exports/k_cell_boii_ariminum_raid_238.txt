(-238 or -237) Boii and Transalpine Gauls make a combined assault on the Roman colony of Ariminum; it fails through dissension born of distrust within the Gallic ranks, which leads the Boii to murder their own kings, and a Roman army sent to meet the threat is speedily withdrawn (Polybius). In the annalistic version (Zonaras) the raid is a Gallic counter-offensive to the Roman attacks of 238 and 237; the Romans then drop the Ligurian campaign, carry the war into Boii territory and confiscate a large part of their land (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3).

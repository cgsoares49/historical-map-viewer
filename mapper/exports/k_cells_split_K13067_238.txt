(-238) Romans attack Gauls (Boii) who are in alliance with Transalpine Gauls (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3).
(-238) Romans begin annual campaigns in Liguria against mountain tribes (-230) (NCAH VII_2.9 pp 432-3) (CAH VII.25 pp 805-6).
(-238) Rebel Punic mercenaries on Sardinia driven out by the natives to Italy; they appeal to Rome which reverses her previous decision and occupies Sardinia before Carthaginians can send a force to do so herself and Rome threatens war if she resists so she acquiesces to the loss, with bitterness. Rome also annexes Corsica (AI) (NCAH VII_2.11 p 569) (CAH VII.25 pp 804-5).

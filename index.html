► New to the Atlas?
What is Mapper?
Mapper is an interactive historical atlas covering the entire world over a span of 2,673 years of political history from 2400 BCE thru the current coverage limit of 272 BCE. Every coloured region represents a real polity at a specific point in history.
Where do I start?
Click Open the Atlas, then type a year into the Date box (e.g. 44 for 44 CE, or -500 for 500 BCE) and press Display. The map will render the political world for that year.
How do I navigate the map?
Drag to pan • Scroll to zoom • Double-click to zoom in • Right-click any region to identify it.
How do I travel through time?
Use the timeline slider at the bottom of the screen, type a new year in the Date box, or press the ⇒ button to animate forward automatically.
What dates are covered?
The atlas currently covers 2400 BCE to 272 BCE. Historical content is being added continuously — earlier periods have broader brushstrokes, while the classical and medieval eras are the most detailed.
What You Can Do
🗺️

2,673 Years of History

Browse political boundaries from 2400 BCE through 272 BCE at any level of magnification. Every border, every empire, rendered from primary historical data.

🎙️

Navigate by Voice or Text

Type or say "show Athens at 450 BCE" and the map flies there instantly. Vibe Map understands natural language commands for dates, places, and zoom.

⏱️

Animate Through Time

Run forward or backward through history at any speed. Watch the ancient world transform — year by year, decade by decade.

🔍

Click Any Country

Right-click any region to see its name, historical date range, and reference links. Every coloured area is identified.

📐

Measure Distances

Alt-click two points to measure great-circle distance in kilometres and miles — useful for understanding the scale of ancient campaigns.

💾

Save Maps & Movies

Export any view as a high-resolution PNG or SVG using a standard Equirectangular or a Lambert Conic projection. Record animated sequences as video to share or embed.

Getting Started
1

Set a Date

Type a year in the Date box, or drag the timeline slider at the bottom of the screen.

2

Find a Place

Type a place name in the Vibe Map box and press Enter, or use the Lon/Lat inputs and click Display.

3

Explore

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Right-click any region to identify it. Double-click to zoom in.

4

Travel Through Time

Press ⇒ to animate forward, or type "run forward" in the Vibe Map box.

Map Controls
Drag

Pan the map; re-renders at new centre on release

Scroll

Zoom in or out, centred on the cursor

Double-click

Zoom in centred on the click point

Right-click

Show country name, date range, and references

Shift+click

Enter selection mode — drag the rectangle, then press Display to render

Alt+click

Drop a distance point; Alt+click again to measure

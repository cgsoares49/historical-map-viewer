OK, up until now we've only used Digitizer within the special realm of bringing in externally created maps, and picking points and un-projecting, if necessary, the maps we've brought in. This has been very useful but it's only a relatively small part of the creation procedure. What I do much more often is to take a map that I have rendered and use an internal version of a digitizer to pick points off of the rendered map and save them in a file. This is most often used for depicting a "transient" event, such as a naval expedition or a movement of an army. I would like you to plan a means to integrate Digitizer that it can be invoked within the Creator app to digitize and save points from a displayed map. I want it to have the same pan and zoom features that we have now in Digitizer or alternatively we can use the existing pan and zoom features within Creator.
